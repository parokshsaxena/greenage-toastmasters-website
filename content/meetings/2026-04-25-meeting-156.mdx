## About This Meeting

Friends roast you without mercy and still stand by you when it matters most. They call out your flaws, pull your leg, and sometimes get you into trouble — but they're also your biggest support. In a world of surface-level connections, true friendship is built on honesty, trust, and showing up for each other. Because the ones who laugh at you the hardest are often the ones who stand with you the strongest.
