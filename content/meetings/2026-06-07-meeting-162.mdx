## About This Meeting

**Speaking Wizards 4.0** — *Say it like you mean it!*

After three successful seasons, Greenage Toastmasters opened its doors wide for the fourth edition of its signature impromptu speaking contest. This wasn't a regular members-only meeting — it was a city-wide open event welcoming anyone aged 18 and above.

The format was simple and delightfully unpredictable: participants received a completely random topic on the spot and had 2 minutes to speak their mind. No preparation. No scripts. Just raw, spontaneous expression.

The evening was a celebration of courage — first-timers stepping up alongside seasoned speakers, every participant cheered on by an enthusiastic and supportive crowd. No winners, no losers — just everyone finding their voice.

*"There are no mistakes here, only learning experiences."*
