## About This Meeting

Imagine waking up one day and realising AI knows your voice, your habits, your choices… maybe even better than your closest friends. Exciting? Terrifying? Or both?

AI is rapidly transforming how we work, communicate, create, and think — and the change is happening faster than ever before. Meeting #160 explored the evolving relationship between technology and humanity through powerful speeches, engaging Table Topics, and thought-provoking conversations.

The central question of the evening: **Is AI humanity's greatest assistant, or could it become our biggest challenge?**

Through every speech and discussion, one theme kept surfacing — the human qualities of empathy, creativity, and connection that will always remain irreplaceable, no matter how fast the machines learn.
