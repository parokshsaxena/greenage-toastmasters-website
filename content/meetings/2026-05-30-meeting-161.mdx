## About This Meeting

*"Veg"* was not enough… so who invented *"Pure Veg"*? Someone, somewhere, looked at perfectly fine vegetables and thought — *needs more purity?*

Meeting #161 took a sharp, witty look at the world of marketing through the lens of everyday language. From **Pure Veg 🍀** to **Ultra Luxury Apartments 🏢** to **AI-Powered Everything 🤖** — we uncovered how one extra adjective can completely transform trust, emotion, and perception.

The session was packed with:
- Sharp humour and witty observations
- Impromptu Table Topics that had everyone nodding a little too hard
- And an honest reckoning with all the marketing madness we've all fallen for — at least once

The takeaway? Sometimes we're not buying the product. We're buying the extra adjective.
